ChatGPT is an artificial intelligence (AI) chatbot developed by OpenAI.
 It can understand natural language and generate human-like responses to questions and prompts.
  ChatGPT can be used for many purposes, such as learning, writing, coding, brainstorming, summarizing information, and solving problems. 
   It can explain complex topics in simple language and help users explore different ideas. However, ChatGPT can sometimes make mistakes, 
   so important information should be verified from reliable sources. Overall, ChatGPT is a useful AI tool for learning, productivity,
    and everyday assistance.
